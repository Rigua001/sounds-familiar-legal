Privacy Policy

Sounds Familiar Publisher (the "App") - Effective 14 October 2026

1. Who this covers

The App is a private tool run by the owner of the "Sounds Familiar" channel (the "Operator") to post the Operator's own videos to the Operator's own social media accounts. It has no public users. The only personal data it handles belongs to the Operator and to the accounts the Operator connects.

2. Data the App receives from connected platforms

  • Account identification: the connected account's identifier, username, display name and, where the platform provides it, avatar image address. This is used to show which account is connected and to address uploads to it.
  • Access credentials: the OAuth access token (and refresh token, where issued) that the platform grants when the Operator connects an account, with its expiry time and the permissions granted.
  • Upload results: the identifier, address and processing status of each video the App posts.

The App does not receive or store followers, friends, messages, comments, analytics about other people, or any content other than what the Operator uploads.

3. Where data is stored

All of the above is stored only on the Operator's own computer and private network storage, in files and a local database that the Operator controls. Access credentials are kept in a local credentials file and are never written to a database, sent to any third party, or included in logs or error reports.

4. How data is used

  • To sign the Operator in to a connected account and keep that connection working (token refresh).
  • To upload videos the Operator has approved, with their captions, to the connected account, and to check whether the upload finished.
  • To show the Operator the status of each post inside the App.

Data is never sold, rented, shared with advertisers, or used for profiling. The App makes no automated decisions about any person.

5. Sharing

The App sends data only to the platform the data belongs to: a video and its caption go to the platform it is being posted to, using that platform's official API, together with the access token that platform issued. No other recipient receives any data from the App.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Access credentials are kept until the Operator disconnects the account in the App, which deletes them, or until they expire.
  • Revoking the App's access from within TikTok, Instagram or Facebook settings also ends the App's ability to use the credentials; any remaining local copy is deleted at the next connection attempt.
  • Records of posted videos (identifiers, status) are kept as the Operator's own publishing history and can be deleted by the Operator at any time.

To request deletion of any data held by the App, contact the address below; the Operator will delete it within 30 days.

7. Security

Credentials are stored with restricted file permissions on the Operator's machine, all platform communication uses HTTPS, and secrets are redacted from logs. No system is perfectly secure; the Operator takes reasonable steps to protect the data described here.

8. Children

The App is not directed at anyone under 18 and does not knowingly process data about children.

9. Third-party platforms

TikTok, Instagram, Facebook and YouTube process data under their own privacy policies when the App interacts with them. Those policies, not this one, govern what the platforms do with posted videos and account data.
